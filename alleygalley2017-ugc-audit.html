How posts were classified

A post is counted as UGC when its caption shows product-marketing signals: an @brand tag, TikTok Shop hashtags (#tiktokshop, #dealsforyoudays), disclosed sponsorship (#ad, #partner, #ambassador, gifted / "sent to me"), or sales language ("grab yours", "add to cart", discount codes, "sold out"). UGC-leaning posts have a single soft product mention. Organic posts are personal/family/lifestyle or follow-for-follow growth posts with no product. It's a heuristic — use the search + filters below to verify any post against its caption.
